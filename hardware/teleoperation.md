## Leader arms

- [GELLO](https://github.com/wuphilipp/gello_mechanical) - 3D-printed leader arms matching Franka, UR, xArm and other robots, with STLs, priced BOM and companion [software](https://github.com/wuphilipp/gello_software) (MIT). 🖨️ 💵

The SO-101 and Koch v1.1 leader arms ship with their follower arms; see [arms.md](arms.md).

## Handheld interfaces and exoskeletons

- [Universal Manipulation Interface](https://github.com/real-stanford/universal_manipulation_interface) - Handheld GoPro gripper and pipeline for collecting demonstrations without a robot; design files via the hardware guide, no separate hardware license (code MIT). 🖨️

## VR, phone and game controllers

- [teleop](https://github.com/SpesRobotics/teleop) - Control a ROS 2 arm from a phone or VR headset via WebXR pose tracking with no extra hardware (Apache-2.0). 💵

LeRobot's built-in gamepad, keyboard and phone teleoperators are covered in [learning-frameworks.md](../software/learning-frameworks.md); XLeRobot's Quest 3, Joy-Con and Xbox scripts in [mobile-robots.md](mobile-robots.md).
