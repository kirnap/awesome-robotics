## Leader arms

- [Koch v1.1 leader](https://github.com/jess-moss/koch-v1-1) - Passive leader arm matching the Koch follower for puppeteering-style demonstrations. 🧩 🖨️ 💵
- [SO-101 leader](https://github.com/TheRobotStudio/SO-ARM100) - Geared-down leader arm matching the SO-101 follower, the most common LeRobot data-collection setup. 🧩 🖨️ 💵

## VR and game controllers

- [XLeRobot teleop scripts](https://github.com/Vector-Wangel/XLeRobot) - Keyboard, Xbox controller, Nintendo Joy-Con and Meta Quest 3 VR control for a dual-arm mobile robot, in sim and on hardware. 🧩
- [LeRobot teleoperators](https://github.com/huggingface/lerobot) - Built-in support for gamepads, keyboards and phones as teleoperation devices alongside leader arms. 🧩
