## Leader arms

- [GELLO](https://github.com/wuphilipp/gello_mechanical) - Low-cost 3D-printed leader arms that kinematically match Franka, UR and xArm robots, with companion [software](https://github.com/wuphilipp/gello_software). 🖨️ 💵
- [Koch v1.1 leader](https://github.com/jess-moss/koch-v1-1) - Passive leader arm matching the Koch follower for puppeteering-style demonstrations. 🧩 🖨️ 💵
- [SO-101 leader](https://github.com/TheRobotStudio/SO-ARM100) - Geared-down leader arm matching the SO-101 follower, the most common LeRobot data-collection setup. 🧩 🖨️ 💵

## Handheld and robot-free data collection

- [Universal Manipulation Interface](https://github.com/real-stanford/universal_manipulation_interface) - Handheld 3D-printed gripper with a GoPro that collects in-the-wild demonstrations without a robot, then transfers to real arms. 🖨️ 💵

## VR, phone and game controllers

- [LeRobot teleoperators](https://github.com/huggingface/lerobot) - Built-in support for gamepads, keyboards and phones as teleoperation devices alongside leader arms. 🧩
- [Open-TeleVision](https://github.com/OpenTeleVision/TeleVision) - Immersive VR teleoperation for humanoids and bimanual arms using Vision Pro or Quest with active stereo vision.
- [teleop](https://github.com/SpesRobotics/teleop) - Control any ROS 2 arm from a phone's WebXR pose tracking, no extra hardware required. 💵
- [XLeRobot teleop scripts](https://github.com/Vector-Wangel/XLeRobot) - Keyboard, Xbox controller, Nintendo Joy-Con and Meta Quest 3 VR control for a dual-arm mobile robot, in sim and on hardware. 🧩
