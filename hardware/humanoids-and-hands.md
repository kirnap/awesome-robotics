## Humanoids

- [Berkeley Humanoid Lite](https://github.com/HybridRobotics/berkeley-humanoid-lite) - Humanoid with 3D-printed cycloidal gearboxes, Onshape CAD, print files, BOM and Isaac Lab training code (code MIT, assets CC BY-SA 4.0). 🖨️
- [K-Bot](https://github.com/kscalelabs/kbot) - K-Scale Labs full-size humanoid with published mechanical and electrical sources and the [ksim](https://github.com/kscalelabs/ksim) RL library (hardware CERN-OHL-S-2.0).

## Bipeds

- [Open Duck Mini](https://github.com/apirrone/Open_Duck_Mini) - 3D-printed biped inspired by Disney's BDX droids with Onshape CAD, BOM and RL walking policies; assembly guide marked incomplete (code Apache-2.0). 🖨️ 💵
- [Upkie](https://github.com/upkie/upkie) - Wheeled biped built from mjbots actuators and a Raspberry Pi, with step-by-step build docs, BOM and PyBullet Gymnasium environments (Apache-2.0). 🖨️

## Quadrupeds

- [Stanford Pupper](https://github.com/Nate711/pupperv3-monorepo) - Stanford's 3D-printed educational quadruped, now at v3 with ROS 2 and RL policies (GPL-3.0; the [original Pupper code](https://github.com/stanfordroboticsclub/StanfordQuadruped) is MIT). 🖨️

See also the Solo quadruped designs in the [Open Dynamic Robot Initiative](components.md#motor-controllers-and-actuators) entry.

## Dexterous hands

- [LEAP Hand](https://github.com/leap-hand/LEAP_Hand_API) - Low-cost 3D-printed anthropomorphic hand from CMU with 16 Dynamixel-driven joints, widely used in dexterous manipulation research (CC BY-NC 4.0, noncommercial). 🖨️
