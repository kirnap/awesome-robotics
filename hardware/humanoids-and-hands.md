## Humanoids

- [AgiBot X1](https://github.com/AgibotTech/agibot_x1_hardware) - Open hardware release of AgiBot's full-size modular humanoid, with CAD, BOM and actuator specs.
- [Berkeley Humanoid Lite](https://github.com/HybridRobotics/Berkeley-Humanoid-Lite) - Open-source 3D-printed humanoid designed to be built for under $5,000 with off-the-shelf parts and cycloidal gearboxes. 🖨️
- [HopeJR](https://github.com/TheRobotStudio/HOPEJr) - Open humanoid upper body from The Robot Studio with arms and dexterous hands, supported by LeRobot. 🧩 🖨️
- [K-Bot](https://github.com/kscalelabs/kbot) - K-Scale Labs' open-source full-size humanoid, trained with the companion [ksim](https://github.com/kscalelabs/ksim) JAX reinforcement learning stack.
- [Poppy Humanoid](https://github.com/poppy-project/poppy-humanoid) - The original 3D-printed open humanoid from Inria, built on Dynamixel servos for research and education. 🖨️
- [Reachy Mini](https://github.com/pollen-robotics/reachy_mini) - Open-hardware expressive desktop robot from Pollen Robotics and Hugging Face, built for LeRobot and Hub integration. 🧩 💵

## Bipeds

- [Open Duck Mini](https://github.com/apirrone/Open_Duck_Mini) - 3D-printed open bipedal robot inspired by Disney's BDX droids, walking with RL policies trained in MuJoCo. 🖨️ 💵

## Quadrupeds

- [MIT Mini Cheetah](https://github.com/mit-biomimetics/Cheetah-Software) - Control software for the MIT Mini Cheetah, the reference design behind a generation of low-cost research quadrupeds.
- [Open Dynamic Robot Initiative](https://github.com/open-dynamic-robot-initiative/open_robot_actuator_hardware) - Open-hardware actuator modules and the Solo quadruped from Max Planck and NYU, with full CAD and BOM.
- [SpotMicro](https://github.com/mike4192/spotMicro) - ROS implementation for the 3D-printed SpotMicro quadruped, a staple of hobbyist legged robotics. 🖨️ 💵
- [Stanford Pupper](https://github.com/stanfordroboticsclub/StanfordQuadruped) - Stanford's low-cost 3D-printed quadruped for education, with the newer [Pupper v3](https://github.com/Nate711/pupperv3-monorepo) running ROS 2 and RL policies. 🖨️

## Dexterous hands and grippers

- [HopeJR Hand](https://github.com/TheRobotStudio/HOPEJr) - 3D-printed tendon-driven dexterous hand that ships as part of the HopeJR humanoid. 🧩 🖨️
- [LEAP Hand](https://github.com/leap-hand/LEAP_Hand_API) - Low-cost 3D-printed anthropomorphic hand from CMU with 16 Dynamixel-driven DOF, widely used in dexterous manipulation research. 🖨️
- [ORCA Hand](https://github.com/orcahand/orca_core) - Open-source tendon-driven dexterous hand from ETH Zurich with control core, CAD and teleop tooling. 🖨️
- [RUKA](https://github.com/ruka-hand/RUKA) - NYU's 3D-printed tendon-driven humanoid hand, built for around $1,300 and controlled by learned policies. 🖨️
